About Us

Louie’s Cocktail Lounge is a karaoke bar, live music venue and sports bar in Rancho Cordova, one block north of Mather Field Rd just off Highway 50 at exit 15. Our doors are open from 6am to 2am, every single day of the year.

Two happy hours daily — 6 to 10 in the morning and 4 to 7 in the evening — with a different drink special every night of the week. Our stage is worked by some of the best KJs in Sacramento, and with a professional live stage and a proper dance floor, the spotlight is yours for the taking.

Thirteen HD widescreens for the big game, professional pool tables, darts, pinball and electronic game machines. Surround sound from the Music Box, a heated outdoor patio with misters for summer, and a hundred camera-secured parking spaces right in front.

What’s on through the week

  • Karaoke Wednesday through Saturday, 9pm to 1:30am, no cover.
  • Bingo every Tuesday at 7:30pm, free to play.
  • 8-ball pool tournament every Saturday at 3pm, $10 buy-in, winner takes all.
  • Live bands and DJs most weekends — see the calendar for who’s playing.
  • Every game — NFL, MLB, NBA, NCAA, soccer and golf on satellite.

Getting here

We’re the easiest bar in Sacramento County to get to. One block north of Mather Field Rd, off exit 15 on Highway 50 — easy on, easy off, and no crawling through city traffic. Free parking out front, a hundred spaces, all on camera.

Booking the venue

Louie’s hosts birthdays, celebrations of life, club runs, car shows and company parties. If you’re a band or a KJ looking for a room in the Sacramento area, get in touch — we book live entertainment most weeks.

Club rules

  • Dress code strictly enforced Friday and Saturday nights after 9pm.
  • IDs checked by security from 9pm on Friday and Saturday. You must have valid ID to enter.
  • Last call 1:30am. Complimentary water.
  • Soda available for purchase until 1:55am.

Dress code

  • No durags, bandanas or hoods up.
  • No attire or colours affiliated with any gang or club, support attire included.
  • No saggy or baggy clothing. Pants and shorts must be appropriately sized and held at the waist.
  • No tank tops on men.

We reserve the right to refuse service or entry to anyone. Must be 21 or over.

Also good to know

  • ATM on site if you prefer to pay cash.
  • Free WiFi — ask the bartender for details.
  • Parking — a huge lot with plenty of free spaces.
